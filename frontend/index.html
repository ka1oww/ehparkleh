EhParkLeh: find parking near you in Singapore

Search a destination or use Near me to list carparks around you on a map. Compare them by live lot counts, rates, parking rules, and amenities. The dataset covers more than 3,500 carparks across Singapore, from HDB estates and URA street lots to malls and private car parks.

Live carpark availability

Lot counts come from the data.gov.sg carpark availability feed and refresh about once a minute. Each count is labelled Live, Recent, or Saved, so an older number is never shown as current. Carparks the feed does not cover carry a No live data chip instead of a guess.

HDB, mall, street, and private parking

Filter results to HDB estates, shopping malls, street carparks, or private car parks, and narrow the search radius. An OpenStreetMap layer adds further parking spots on unfiltered searches; it is hidden while a filter is on so nothing appears as a match the data cannot verify.

Rates and free Sunday parking

Every carpark card shows its resolved rate where one is known, along with parsed free-parking rules. One filter keeps only carparks that are free on Sundays and public holidays.

EV charging

Filter to carparks with EV charging. Site flags and connector counts come from LTA DataMall, with live connector availability when that feed is configured.

What EhParkLeh does not do

EhParkLeh finds parking. It does not handle payment, booking, or accounts. The app is free to use.
